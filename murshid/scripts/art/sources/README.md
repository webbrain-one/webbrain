# Sources for the homepage pictures

`../halftone.py` prints these pictures as the gold halftone on the Murshid
homepage. Every one is in the public domain. Only public-domain pictures may be
added here.

Each file is the part of the picture the homepage uses, cut from Wikimedia
Commons' 1920-pixel-wide rendering of the original (downloaded 2026-11-06). The
crop is given as left, top, right, bottom in that rendering's pixels, so the
full picture can be fetched again from the link.

## theologian-with-koran-osman-hamdi-1902.jpg

- **What:** *Islamic Theologian with the Quran* (*İlahiyatçı*): a scholar in a
  white turban and a yellow robe, reading at a Quran stand. The hero picture,
  and the "Ask & act" card.
- **Artist:** Osman Hamdi Bey (1842–1910). **Date:** 1902.
- **Held by:** Belvedere, Vienna (inv. 3077).
- **From:** <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Osman_Hamdi_Bey_-_Islamischer_Theologe_mit_Koran_-_3077_-_Kunsthistorisches_Museum.jpg>
  (original 3508 × 2942). Crop 560, 0, 1810, 1600.
- **Licence:** public domain. Commons tags: `PD-Art`, `PD-old-100-expired`.

## dervish-vanmour-rijksmuseum-sk-a-2030.jpg

- **What:** *A Dervish* (*Een derwisj*): a Mevlevi dervish in a white robe,
  right arm raised, turning. One of the Vanmour series of Ottoman figures. The
  picture beside "The way".
- **Artist:** anonymous, of the Vanmour series. **Date:** 1700–1737.
- **Held by:** Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam, SK-A-2030.
- **From:** <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Derwisj_Rijksmuseum_SK-A-2030.jpeg>
  (original 5024 × 6270). Crop 420, 80, 1900, 2200.
- **Licence:** public domain. The Rijksmuseum publishes its pictures of
  public-domain works without restrictions.

## public-scribe-osman-hamdi-1910.jpg

- **What:** the tiled lunette and grilled window from *Public Scribe*
  (*Arzuhalci*). The "Browse" card.
- **Artist:** Osman Hamdi Bey. **Date:** 1910.
- **Held by:** Sakıp Sabancı Museum, Istanbul.
- **From:** <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Osman_Hamdi_Bey_-_Arzuhalci_,_Public_Scribe_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg>
  (original 4193 × 5945, from Google Arts & Culture). Crop 420, 240, 1480, 902.
- **Licence:** public domain. Commons tags: `PD-Art`, `PD-old-100-expired`.

## dervishes-with-instruments-rijksmuseum-rp-f-00-6419.jpg

- **What:** from *Dervishes with instruments, Constantinople*: a dervish
  playing the kanun and another with a ney. Albumen print. The "Tray" and "New
  tab" cards.
- **Photographer:** anonymous. **Date:** about 1870–1900.
- **Held by:** Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam, RP-F-00-6419.
- **From:** <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Derwisjen_met_instrumenten,_Constantinopel_(huidige_Istanboel),_Ottomaanse_Rijk_(huidige_Turkije),_RP-F-00-6419.jpg>
  (original 3600 × 2930). Crop 120, 560, 1460, 1072.
- **Licence:** CC0 (the Rijksmuseum's dedication), and public domain by age.

## tortoise-trainer-osman-hamdi-1906.jpg

- **What:** the tortoises at the old man's feet in *The Tortoise Trainer*
  (*Kaplumbağa Terbiyecisi*). The "Local" card.
- **Artist:** Osman Hamdi Bey. **Date:** 1906.
- **Held by:** Pera Museum, Istanbul.
- **From:** <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Osman_Hamdi_Bey_-_The_Tortoise_Trainer_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg>
  (original 9492 × 17758, from Google Arts & Culture). Crop 60, 2830, 1260, 3580.
- **Licence:** public domain. Commons tags: `PD-Art`, `PD-old-100-expired`.

## dancing-dervishes-istanbul-rijksmuseum-rp-f-f01141-ch.jpg

- **What:** from *Group of dancing dervishes in Istanbul*: whirling dervishes
  with their arms raised before a wooden lodge, from a travel album of 1910.
  Albumen print. The "Open" card.
- **Photographer:** anonymous. **Date:** about 1900, before April 1910.
- **Held by:** Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam, RP-F-F01141-CH.
- **From:** <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Groep_dansende_derwisjen_in_Istanbul,_RP-F-F01141-CH.jpg>
  (original 3160 × 2542). Crop 20, 330, 1000, 942.
- **Licence:** CC0 (the Rijksmuseum's dedication), and public domain by age.
