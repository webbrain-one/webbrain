# Sources for the traced artwork

`../trace.py` traces these two pictures into the line drawings on the Murshid
homepage. Both are in the public domain. Only public-domain pictures may be
added here.

## suleiman-nakkas-osman-1579.jpg

- **What:** Suleiman the Magnificent, seated, in a white turban. Ottoman
  miniature from the *Şemailname* (portraits of the Ottoman sultans).
- **Artist:** Nakkaş Osman. **Date:** 1579.
- **Held by:** Topkapı Palace Museum, Istanbul, MS Hazine 1563, fol. 47v.
- **From:** Wikimedia Commons,
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Istanbul,_Topkapi_Palace_Museum_MS_Hazine_1563_fol._47v_Suleyman_I_by_Nakka%C5%9F_Osman.jpg>
  (1116 × 1868, downloaded 2026-11-06; re-saved here as JPEG quality 90).
- **Licence:** public domain. Commons tags: `PD-US-expired`, `PD-old-70` (the
  artist died more than 70 years ago).

## semazen-mevlevi-lithograph-bm-1904-0929-59.jpg

- **What:** *A Mevlevee, or turning Dervish* ("Derviche Tourneur"): a
  whirling dervish, right hand raised, skirt flared. Hand-coloured lithograph.
- **Artist:** unknown (lithographic print). **Date:** about 1830–1860.
- **Held by:** British Museum, Prints and Drawings, 1904,0929.59.
- **From:** Wikimedia Commons,
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Derviche_Tourneur_(BM_1904,0929.59).jpg>
  (1165 × 1600, downloaded 2026-11-06, unchanged).
- **Licence:** public domain; the print is about 170 years old. Commons tag:
  `PD-scan`. The British Museum labels its own photograph CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
  Commons treats a faithful photograph of a flat public-domain work as public
  domain too. Either way, the homepage carries only an outline drawn from the
  print, not the photograph.

## Not used

The Correr Museum miniature of Suleiman that inspired the portrait is not on
Commons, so the 1579 Topkapı portrait above was traced in its place. It is the
